# Transporter shader verification

Run the focused controller and lifecycle tests:

```sh
node --experimental-vm-modules tests/transporter-shader.mjs
```

Run the isolated WebGL/controls fixture page:

```sh
node tests/serve-transporter-tests.cjs
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:3188/tests/transporter-browser.html and choose **Run rendering checks**. The server uses the installed Foundry `QuadMesh`, `AbstractBaseShader`, and PIXI; it does not connect to a world. Pass a different Foundry `resources/app` directory as the optional command argument. **Hold midpoint**, **Beam In**, **Beam Out**, and **Preview native fallback** allow visual inspection of all eleven presets on transparent figures and portraits. The form below is the actual Transporter configuration template with a private settings store.

The node suite covers normalized settings and timing, Voyager's two successive orb pairs, TMP's grow-then-separate lines, Romulan's downward/outward/inward scans, cancellation/replacement, document-free previews, fallback, context loss, scene teardown, delayed/missing tokens, duplicate/expired/socket messages, hidden tokens, six concurrent departures, cross-scene completion, and failed document operations. The browser suite checks 324 rendered shader frames, pixel-level uniform token fading and field reveal behind the scan, transformed artwork, preservation of another filter, cleanup, unsaved form previews, Stop/Reset, and settings persistence in its isolated store. Use the sequence slider to inspect scan travel and line growth/separation at different points.

## Live-world acceptance

Dancing-particle presets also include a second spherical cluster centered on the artwork, with half the outer cylinder's width at the default size (limited by the artwork's shorter dimension to stay round). Inner cluster size (%) adjusts this sphere's spread from 25 to 200; 100 preserves the original size. Check both limits, Preview, Save/reopen and Reset. Individual particle size and the outer cylinder remain unchanged. Nested orbits fill its interior, with bright front particles and dim rear particles. Inspect tall figures and square portraits in both directions. Inner cluster intensity and Outer dancing particle intensity independently control brightness from 0 to 1; zero disables the corresponding layer. Old saves retain their previous shared brightness until an independent inner intensity is saved. Preview, Save and Reset should apply both controls separately. Inner cluster direction and Outer particle direction independently choose clockwise or counterclockwise rotation viewed from above; test opposite directions, unsaved Preview, Save/reopen and Reset. Their upward drift and gentle bobbing are preserved. Inner dancing particle speed and Outer dancing particle speed are independent (0–4); zero pauses only that layer. Old saved shared speeds initialize both controls equally. Verify different speeds, either layer paused while the other moves, Preview, Save/reopen and Reset. Both layers share size, detail and shimmer controls. The sphere remains clipped to artwork transparency. Romulan, Borg and Dominion retain their field, strand and haze effects.

All eleven cinematic presets include stationary sparkles with independent short appearances at scattered positions inside the artwork. Each sparkle holds its position and size while visible, then disappears; later births choose new positions. **Static sparkle amount** (0–1, default 0.55) sets density, with zero disabling the layer; **Static sparkle rate** (0.1–4, default 1.5) controls frequency independently of particle travel and shimmer. Romulan sparkles follow its scan reveal. Tests isolate this layer in every preset to check popping, deterministic replay, transparent-pixel masking, disabling, unsaved preview, Save, and Reset. It uses the existing token filter and cleanup timeline without additional graphics objects or document writes.

**Dominion** (`dominion`) starts with a soft haze, then introduces dense near-token-height rain. Primary defaults to pale blue, Secondary to lavender, and Highlight to white; lanes retain separate colors. Preview/save/reset all three colors. Tune Rain density/speed and Haze detail/intensity/motion, and verify a clean fade in both directions. It has an independent optional sound slot. The native fallback provides a simplified mist column and mixed-color streaks.

**Enterprise Era** (`entFed`) shares the TOS shader choreography and timing, with pale blue `#c4e5ff` and white `#f5fbff` highlights. Select it from the transporter emitter menu; tune it and assign its optional sound under Sounds & Animations → Transporter. Confirm its circular outline and depth particles match TOS while its colors and saved tuning remain independent.

TMP/Films has **Swipe height (%)** (25–200) and **Swipe width (%)** (25–300) controls. Both default to 100, work in unsaved previews, persist with Save, and return to 100 with Reset Preset. Verify the pointed tips and broad glow remain unclipped at maximum size. Borg uses independently angled, curved energy strands with random positions and separate pulses. Inspect both directions and confirm the strands cross at varied angles rather than forming parallel columns. Its thickness, density, intensity, motion speed, and glow can be tuned in the preset controls.

Romulan uses a fine field following artwork transparency. A bright horizontal scan descends during 4–30% of the sequence, establishing the field behind it. Two faint vertical scans spread from the center during 33–50%, then return intensely during 50–73% and fade by 80%. Check the early scan, faint spread, bright convergence, and cleanup in both beam directions. Tune Field texture width, Field detail, Field intensity, Field motion speed, and Glow. Existing saved colors and tuning still apply; Reset Preset restores defaults.

After reloading Foundry, choose **Sounds & Animations → Transporter → Cinematic Shader**, then Save. Use a disposable test scene for actual departures and arrivals.

- Preview TNG/Voyager, TOS, and TMP on actual figure and portrait artwork. Confirm the artwork fades evenly beneath dancing sparkles and a colored overlay, TNG/Voyager rain reads as falling streaks, Voyager's second orb pair follows the first, and TMP's two lines reach full height before moving apart. TOS/TMP should have complete circular outlines with no clipping. Stop/closing the settings window must restore original opacity and other filters.
- With a GM and player viewing the same scene, transport one token and then six. The matter transition finishes at 80% of the preset duration; only the GM writes documents. Check a hidden token and a player without sight of a token.
- Beam queued actors into a free placement, a formation, and transporter pads. Beam them out, switch scenes, and restore their buffer group; check wildcard artwork and linked actors.
- In each destination pad's Region configuration, set **Force Beam-In Emitter** to **TNG Federation** and save. Reopen to verify persistence. Beam out with **Klingon**, switch to the destination scene, select its pad group, and restore both a single buffer group and Restore All. Arrivals should use TNG effects and sound. Repeat with a queued beam-in. Clear the override to **Use buffer / selected emitter** and confirm buffered Klingon patterns arrive using Klingon again. Run `node tests/transporter-pad-emitter.mjs` for isolated regression coverage.
- Switch to each existing engine and repeat one arrival/departure with its usual sound. Confirm the shader controls did not alter the previous engine's effects.
- Change duration, preview without saving, cancel, reopen, and verify saved values. Confirm engine selection remains unchanged until Save.

Live-world behavior cannot be fully established by the isolated fixture page: it uses ordinary PIXI sprites and mocked token documents, rather than Foundry's entire vision/occlusion, TokenDocument replication, and placement workflow.

Particle detail review: dancing lights should vary from tiny to small circular orbs, with gentle size changes as each moves into and out of apparent depth. Look for uneven clusters, empty patches, overlapping glints, independent birth/fade timing, and slowly changing luminous patches rather than a regular dot lattice or synchronized oscillation. Rain trails should span roughly 80–100% of the artwork height before clipping at the effect boundary, without breaks from vertical tiling. TMP's two swipes should be four times the previous center width and taper continuously to points at the top and bottom.

Dancing particles retain the cylindrical trajectories: they circle a vertical axis and rise gently, crossing the front brightly and receding onto a dim rear arc. The particle field now renders through the token filter and is clipped to artwork transparency, including transparent gaps and partially transparent edges. The **Outer dancing particle speed** slider (0–4) controls cylindrical angular and upward motion, while **Inner dancing particle speed** controls the sphere's rotation and bobbing; zero pauses that layer while **Shimmer speed** controls flicker independently. Old saved presets retain their previous movement speed until the new control is saved. Browser checks cover particle masking, motion, deterministic replay, zero-speed pause, independence from shimmer, unsaved speed previews, Save/Reset, and absence of particles on the unmasked overlay. Romulan and Borg retain their distinct field/strand effects.
