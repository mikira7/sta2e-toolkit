Adv. Dice Roller — one look per era

The roller dialog (buildDialogContent in npc-roller.js) wearing each era's chat card. The frames, dice strips, gauges, notes and keys are the cards' own classes, drawn by the real stylesheets linked above; only the roller's form parts — fields, indicators, the Complication range, the payment slots — are new, as .rl-* rules in this page. One scenario throughout: Lt. Worf fires the phaser banks with Data assisting and the Enterprise in the pool. Left is the setup phase, right the rolled phase. The window header is core's DialogV2 chrome and stays as it is; Roll and Cancel move from the dialog footer into the frame.

lcars-tng

TNG Bracket Console

Sections are the card's stadium dice strips. Checkboxes are short LCARS pills lit in the frame colour; selects and the Difficulty field are outlined stadiums; the Complication range is a red segment gauge the slider sits on.
