Romulan actor sheets

The five 2e actor sheets — character, NPC, supporting, starship (Ship and Crew tabs) and small craft — in the Raptor Wing look the task card, chat cards and dice roller now wear under romulan: cold green on a fine grid, diamond separators, nothing round. The record panel wears the card's whole head — the warbird head-on, two swept wings running into the underlined title, the code on a diamond line beneath it — and every other panel takes the diamond line alone. Readouts and lists are the card's black hex wells, the ship's four stats its chamfered cells, tracks are rows of numbered diamonds, selectors are diamonds that light jade, and keys and tabs are the card's dark hex keys edged in their colour. Hover a list row to bring up its edit and delete keys; the tracks and selectors are live.

    How this would be built

    Built: styles/romulan-sheet.css, tagged by tos-sheet-theme.js as .sta2e-rom-sheet and skipped by _applySheetTheme, as described below. In the build the record wings are SVG backgrounds rather than clip-paths, so the title can share their pseudo-element.

    The same route as the TOS, TMP, ENT and Klingon overlays: a CSS overlay, never a template. tos-sheet-theme.js would stamp .sta2e-rom-sheet (one more entry in ERA_CLASSES, under isRomulanTheme) beside the data-sta2e-sheet="<kind>" stamp it already writes, _applySheetTheme would skip a tagged sheet as it skips the others, and a new styles/romulan-sheet.css would follow klingon-sheet.css rule for rule. The panel heads, the wings and the diamond line are pseudo-elements on the template's own columns, as the Klingon header and the ENT ticks are, so builders emit no markup and every data-action keeps working.

    What carries over from the chat card. The gridded black (.trw's two 12px gradients), the swept wings and the underlined title, the diamond line with its code, the hex well with its top and bottom rules, the chamfered readout cell with a frame-green bottom edge, the diamond note lead, and the edged hex key. Oxanium for labels (already bundled in fonts/romulan/) and Share Tech Mono for numbers (in fonts/tmp/).

    Wings once, diamonds everywhere else. The wings mark the record: the top-right panel on a character, NPC or supporting sheet and the right column's head on a starship or small craft (.top-right-column > .panel-head, .right-column > .panel-head). Every other panel takes the quieter diamond line — a short rule, the title, a long rule, three diamonds and the code — so a sheet does not read as a wall of wings. The record's code drops under its title, where the card puts its state.

    Edges are fills and shadows, never borders. Every cut shape is a clip-path, which cuts a border off at the corners, so a well's rules are inset box-shadows, the portrait's rim is the frame's own fill under a chamfered image, and a key's coloured ends are stops in its background gradient — exactly as the card's keys are drawn. Typed fields are underlined with a box-shadow too, since sta.css forces some border colours with !important.

    Colour by meaning. Reputation is frame green, Shields teal, Determination gold, Stress red, Crew Support amber, and the NPC's Personal Threat amber through the data-sta2e-sheet stamp (it reuses box stress). Selected attributes, disciplines, systems and departments turn gold; non-zero breaches turn red through .breaches:not([value="0"]); the NPC category and a checked NPC Ship fill red. Tracks are the card's diamond meter at a larger size, numbered: each diamond is kept square with aspect-ratio, so a 20-step Reputation track shrinks its diamonds evenly instead of squashing them, and a lit one glows in its track's colour.

    The traps the other overlays carry apply here too. sta.css gives several blocks width: 100% as a content box (list headers, .buttons, .text-entry, the supporting improvement rows), so any padding added must come with box-sizing: border-box; radios group by form, so the check page wraps each sheet in its own <form>; and the real templates would be rendered with Foundry's Handlebars beside foundry2.css and sta.css to check it, as the Klingon overlay was.
