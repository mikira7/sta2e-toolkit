Romulan chat cards and dice roller

Three concepts for the romulan theme, worn by the Romulan campaign era: the Working Results task card, the damage cards (ship and ground), the smaller cards (Confirmed, Sensor Contact, Hazard Entry) and the Adv. Dice Roller in each concept's frame. As with the TNG, TOS, TMP, ENT and Klingon concepts, each is one frame shared by every card, with the card type given by the frame colour. Colours start from the romulan tokens in lcars-theme.js; footers carry the After Settlement date the Romulan Stardate HUD already shows (formatRomulanDate). Rules terms stay in English; Rihannsu appears only as flavour, using words the HUD already uses. Cards are shown at Foundry's chat sidebar width.

Roller window height 560px Every roller window is also drag-resizable from its bottom edge.
Concept A · the Stardate HUD, opened out

Raptor Wing

The Romulan HUD's own language — cold green on a fine grid, diamond separators, nothing round — made into a card. The header is the warbird seen head-on: two swept wings rising out of the title. Dice sit in hexagonal wells, readouts are chamfered cells, Successes against Difficulty is a row of diamonds (red for the Difficulty), and the result is a full-width hex plate. Keys are dark hex keys edged in their colour, so arming takes the unfilled path. Closest to what the era already shows, and the cheapest to build.

Concept B · the Tal Shiar file

Tal Shiar Dossier

Every card is an intelligence file on dark slate-green. A hatched classification band in the card colour names the type, the title is set in Roman capitals beside a raptor seal, and the reference line carries redaction bars. Dice sit in a tray between double rules, readouts are ledger rows with dot leaders and a tally, and the result is struck as an ink stamp with its Rihannsu gloss. Keys are filled file-divider tabs with a cut corner, so arming lights them yellow. The most distinctly Romulan of the three, and the quietest in a busy chat log.

Concept C · the warbird bridge

Singularity Console

The Nemesis-era warbird bridge: dark green glass with one great swept corner, gold hairlines, a hatched intake strip, and a recessed screen for the dice. The readout is the ship's quantum singularity — Successes light round a twelve-segment ring, the Difficulty is the red inner arc, and Momentum sits in the core — so a glance says how far past the Difficulty the roll went. Keys are backlit plates rounded on the hull side and filled, so arming lights them yellow. The richest look, and the one that needs the most new CSS.

The roller on small screens

  • One scroller, keys pinned. Every frame is the same column: header (fixed) → screen (the only element that scrolls) → keys (fixed). The screen is flex: 1; min-height: 0; overflow-y: auto, so on a short display it gives way and Roll / Resolve / Cancel never leave the window. This is the .tbr-screen rule roller.css already applies, so it carries straight over.
  • Scroll cues. A fade appears at whichever edge has more content, and a "▼ n more" chip sits above the keys while anything is below the fold — clicking it scrolls one section. The scrollbar is themed per concept (scrollbar-color plus the WebKit pseudo-elements).
  • Jump strip. In the setup phase a sticky strip of section keys (Officer · Pool · Assist · Ship · Options · Traits) sits at the top of the screen; it scrolls to a section and lights the one in view. It is built from the section headers sectionHeader already emits, so the content needs no new markup.
  • Compaction. The window is a size container, and under 520px tall the form tightens: hint lines (small, Complication description) hide and dice shrink to 26px. The window stays drag-resizable from its bottom edge (core's resize handle in Foundry).
  • Try it with the height slider at the top of this page.

Notes for building any of these

  • The working card would follow the Klingon route: a task-card-romulan.js skin from the same skinView, used for romulan whatever taskCardStyle says, keeping the DOM contract (.sta2e-working-actions, .sta2e-card-die dice with their <img>, button classes, data-*). The other cards get a romulanChatCard branch in lcarsChatCard, with the body through _consoleBodyHtml(html, "rom") so tiles, plates, notes and chips map onto A's cells and hex wells, B's ledger rows and trays, or C's screen.
  • The roller becomes a romulan entry in roller-skin.js (FRAMES, FOOTER_PAINT, rollerSkin()) plus styles/roller-romulan.css, drawing only the shared tbr-* classes, as every other era does.
  • Every key keeps its colour inline (--k here). A's keys are edged, not filled, so arming takes _armCardSelection's unfilled path (yellow text and edge). B and C carry data-filled="1", so arming lights them yellow, as shown. A's and B's cut shapes are a clip-path; C's rounding is positional, odd and even children as in the TNG keypad.
  • Dice colours. The romulan primary (#22aa44) is the same green as a success, so a crit would vanish; critDieColour() would return gold (#d8c050) under Romulan, and successDieColour() the brighter #5cf09a — the token green reads dim on black.
  • Built: Concept A ships as task-card-romulan.js / romulanChatCard (body pass prefix rom) and the romulan roller skin, with the scroll cues as wireRollerScroll in roller-skin.js. In the build the chat frame's diamond line is left empty; the task card's carries the state.
  • A's wings, B's hatched band and seal and C's ring are pseudo-elements or background layers in a build, so builders emit no extra markup. C's ring needs --s / --d written inline on the readout; it is a conic-gradient under a two-layer mask, capped at 12 segments.
  • Rihannsu appears only beside an English rules term (mnhei'sahe on a pass) and in the date, all words the Stardate HUD already uses. A world setting could turn them off.
  • The raptor in B's seal is a plain vector drawn for this mockup, not the Star Empire's emblem.
  • Fonts: A Oxanium; B Cinzel, Barlow Condensed and IBM Plex Mono; C Saira Semi Condensed with Share Tech Mono (already bundled in fonts/tmp/). All are Google fonts under the SIL OFL and would be bundled as fonts/klingon/ is.
