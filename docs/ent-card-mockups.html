ENT chat cards

Three concepts for the ent-panel era (2150s, NX-01): the Working Results task card, the damage cards (ship and ground) and the smaller cards (Confirmed, Sensor Contact, Hazard Entry). As with the TNG concepts, each is one frame shared by every card, with the card type given by the frame colour. Every colour comes from the ent-panel tokens in lcars-theme.js plus the amber the ENT Stardate HUD already uses. The era has no stardates, so footers carry the Earth date and time, as the HUD does. Cards are shown at Foundry's chat sidebar width.

Concept A · the bridge stations

Bridge Touchscreen

The NX-01's flat bridge displays: navy glass with a faint grid, thin blue-white line art and chamfered corners. The title sits on a rule with a cut tab for the station code. Dice sit in a bracketed well. Each readout has a hairline bar graph under it, with a tick at the Difficulty. Keys are tabs with a cut corner and a coloured edge. This is the most "on screen" of the three and the lightest in weight.

Concept B · the Stardate HUD, extended

Bulkhead Console

The ENT Stardate HUD made into a card, so the chat and the calendar bar read as one set of hardware. A gunmetal housing with corner screws, a lit strip along the top and a status LED in the card-type colour, and a UESPA ID block holding the station. Everything that is a number sits on a recessed black display in amber; the result is an illuminated placard; the keys are the HUD's raised rounded-rect buttons with a coloured indicator. The most physical of the three, and the closest to what the era already ships.

Concept C · engineering

Systems Status Board

The NX-01 engine room: a charcoal chassis under a striped hazard band whose colour gives the card type, with heavy condensed hull lettering. A column of square status lamps runs down the left (one lit per card state). Dice sit in square sockets, each number is a segmented block meter (orange segments past the Difficulty, hatched for what was lost) and the result is a solid stencilled bar. Keys are chunky light-grey push buttons with a coloured cap light. The boldest of the three, and the one that reads at a glance in a busy chat log.

Notes for building any of these

  • The working card would follow the TOS / TMP / TNG route: a task-card-ent.js skin built from the same skinView, used for ent-panel whatever taskCardStyle says, and keeping the DOM contract (.sta2e-working-actions, .sta2e-card-die dice with their <img>, button classes, data-*). The other cards would get an entChatCard branch in lcarsChatCard, beside tosChatCard and tmpChatCard.
  • Card bodies are written with inline LCARS shapes, so the ENT frame would need a body pass like tosifyBodyHtml: classify tiles, plates, notes and chips by their inline style and strip only what made them LCARS. B could reuse that classifier almost unchanged (its displays play the part of TOS's CRT screens). A and C would map the same classes onto wells and meters.
  • Built: Concept A ships as task-card-ent.js / entChatCard. Its keys are tabs and deliberately not data-filled, so arming takes the unfilled path (yellow outline and text), which is the armed look shown above.
  • Every key keeps its colour inline (--k here). B and C would carry data-filled="1", so arming lights them yellow, as the TOS and TNG keys do. A's cut corner is a clip-path and its outline an inset box-shadow; B's bevel and C's cap light are a box-shadow and a background stop. None of them touches the four properties _armCardSelection owns, and no radius is inline.
  • A draws the frame border by nesting two clipped boxes, because a clip-path cuts a real border off the diagonal. The reroll tray, appended to .sta2e-working-actions, would sit inside the inner box. B's screws and C's hazard band are pseudo-elements in a build, so builders emit no extra markup.
  • The era has no stardates, so the footer carries the Earth date and time the Stardate HUD already shows. ENT ships have polarized hull plating rather than shields; the cards keep the word Shields because that is the rules term, but a per-era label is a cheap option if wanted.
  • Fonts: A uses Rajdhani with Share Tech Mono for numbers (Share Tech Mono is already bundled in fonts/tmp/); B uses the Helvetica/Arial stack the ENT HUD already uses, so it needs no new font; C uses Saira Condensed. All are Google fonts under the SIL OFL and would be bundled as fonts/tng/ is.
