Klingon chat cards

Three concepts for the klingon theme, worn by the Klingon campaign era: the Working Results task card, the damage cards (ship and ground) and the smaller cards (Confirmed, Sensor Contact, Hazard Entry). As with the TNG, TOS, TMP and ENT concepts, each is one frame shared by every card, with the card type given by the frame colour. Colours start from the klingon tokens in lcars-theme.js. Footers carry the Year of Kahless date the Klingon Stardate HUD already shows (formatKlingonDate). Rules terms stay in English; tlhIngan Hol appears only as flavour in codes and seals, the way the HUD's buttons already use it. Cards are shown at Foundry's chat sidebar width.

Concept A · the Stardate HUD, opened out

Tactical Strut

The Klingon HUD's own language made into a card: a crimson strut with an angled cut carries the code, the title runs across black, and a line of angular script sits under it. No curves anywhere. Dice sit in a blade-ended well, readouts are parallelogram cells, Successes against Difficulty is a run of chevrons, and the result is a full-width strut. Keys are dark parallelograms with a coloured edge, like the HUD's buttons. Closest to what the era already shows, and the cheapest to build.

Concept B · the Great Hall

Forged Plate

Ceremony rather than instruments: a hammered iron plate with a rivet in each corner, a blood-red enamel header cut like a blade, and a three-blade mark beside a title set in a heavy blackletter. Dice sit in recessed wells, readouts are engraved ledger lines with a row of blade teeth for Successes against Difficulty, and the result is struck as an enamel seal: Qapla' on a pass, bIlujpu' on a failure. Keys are raised iron studs with an enamel edge. The most distinctive of the three and the heaviest on the page.

Concept C · the film-era bridge

Targeting Scope

The Klingon bridge of the films: a dim red housing round a green phosphor scope with a faint reticle and scanlines, and the triangle everywhere. The header carries the viewer's inverted triangle as a lamp, dice sit in hexagonal cells, Successes and Difficulty are rows of interlocking triangles, and the keys are backlit plates cut wider at the top. The card type changes the scope's phosphor, not the housing: green for a task, gold passed, red damage, amber for sensors and hazards. Most "instrument" of the three, and the most readable at a glance.

Notes for building any of these

  • The working card would follow the TOS / TMP / TNG / ENT route: a task-card-klingon.js skin built from the same skinView, used for klingon whatever taskCardStyle says, keeping the DOM contract (.sta2e-working-actions, .sta2e-card-die dice with their <img>, button classes, data-*). The other cards would get a klingonChatCard branch in lcarsChatCard, with the body going through _consoleBodyHtml(html, "kli") so tiles, plates, notes and chips map onto A's cells and wells, B's recessed wells and ledger rows, or C's scope.
  • Built: Concept C ships as task-card-klingon.js / klingonChatCard, with the body pass's prefix kli. In the build the lamp carries the Difficulty, and a success die on the card is drawn in the scope's green rather than the token olive.
  • Every key keeps its colour inline (--k here). A's keys are edged, not filled, like ENT's tabs, so arming takes _armCardSelection's unfilled path (yellow text and edge). B and C would carry data-filled="1", so arming lights them yellow, as shown. B's bevel and enamel edge are inset box-shadows, which arming never touches; A's and C's angled ends are a clip-path.
  • A's strut, end cap and script line, B's rivets and C's scope corners are pseudo-elements or background layers in a build, so builders emit no extra markup. The script line is invented strokes worn as a mask-image, not pIqaD, so no font licence is involved and it takes each card's colour.
  • B's seal and C's result line use tlhIngan Hol only beside the English rules term (Qapla', bIlujpu'), and the header codes reuse words the HUD already uses (Qu' task, QIH damage, noch sensor, Qob danger). A world setting could turn them off.
  • The three-blade mark in B is a plain vector drawn for this mockup. The module already ships the Klingon Empire emblem in assets/emblems/ if the real mark is wanted.
  • Fonts: A uses Teko and Barlow Condensed; B uses Grenze Gotisch for titles and seals with Barlow Condensed; C uses Chakra Petch with Share Tech Mono for numbers (already bundled in fonts/tmp/). All are Google fonts under the SIL OFL and would be bundled as fonts/tng/ is.
  • The Romulan theme has the same gap and could follow whichever concept is picked here, with its own palette.
