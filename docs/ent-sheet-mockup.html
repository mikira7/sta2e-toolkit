ENT actor sheets

The five 2e actor sheets — character, NPC, supporting, starship (Ship and Crew tabs) and small craft — in the ENT Bridge Touchscreen look the task and chat cards now use under ent-panel: navy glass panes with thin blue lines and bright corner brackets, titles on a rule with a cut tab, tracks as segmented readout bars, square indicators for the selectors, and tab keys with a coloured edge. The sample data is from the 2150s. Hover a list row to bring up its edit and delete keys; the tracks and selectors are live.

    How this would be built

    The same route as the TOS and TMP overlays: a CSS overlay, never a template. tos-sheet-theme.js would stamp .sta2e-ent-sheet (one more entry in ERA_CLASSES) and data-sta2e-sheet="<kind>" on the five sheets under ent-panel with themeCharacterSheet on, _applySheetTheme would skip a tagged sheet as it does the other two, and a styles/ent-sheet.css would hang every rule off that class. Nothing in the system's markup moves, so every data-action and the track renderer keep working.

    What carries over from the chat card. Navy glass with a faint grid, thin blue lines with bright corner brackets, the title on a rule with three ticks and a cut tab, Rajdhani for labels and Share Tech Mono for every number, and tab keys with a coloured edge and a cut corner. The tracks are the card's readout bars split into segments; the attribute, discipline, system and department selectors are square indicators that fill yellow, the same yellow an armed key takes.

    One difference from the card. The card draws its chamfered outline by nesting two clipped boxes, because a clip-path cuts a real border off the diagonal. The system's panels have no inner box to clip, so a panel here keeps a plain 1px outline and the chamfer is suggested by two bright corner brackets. Tabs and keys have no outline to lose, so they keep the real cut corner. The header ticks, the cut tab and the caption under the portrait are markup in this mockup; in a build they would be pseudo-elements, as the TOS header strip is.

    Text fields are underlined, not boxed. NX-01 touchscreen fields are lines, not wells. sta.css forces some border colours with !important, so the underline would be a box-shadow, as the TOS overlay already does.

    The traps the TOS and TMP files carry apply here too. sta.css gives several blocks width: 100% as a content box (list headers, .buttons, .text-entry, the supporting improvement rows), so any padding added must come with box-sizing: border-box. The NPC's Personal Threat reuses box stress, so the data-sheet stamp is what turns it orange. Breaches turn red through .breaches:not([value="0"]), with no script.
