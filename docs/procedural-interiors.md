# Procedural interiors

Planned complete-room puzzle kit: [production inventory and fitting rules](interior-prefab-production-plan.md). This is a pre-production checklist; the existing generator and room images are not yet a fixed-footprint prefab system.

Open **Scenes → Generate Interior**, or use **Generate Interior** in the STA2e Toolkit widget. Only the GM can create scenes.

Choose a starship or station, faction, era, size, and deck architecture. **Faction default** now uses a **Hull section / curved passageway** for non-Borg interiors; Borg keeps its conduit grid. Hull sections choose a room program before sizing the geometry, put cabins and social spaces outboard where possible, and connect every compartment directly to a continuous passageway. Curved outer bulkheads and radial partitions produce tapered rooms; engineering, medical, command, and living spaces have different size profiles. The **Curved hull and passageways** checkbox switches between sampled curves and faceted geometry. The older longitudinal, full-ring, and modular-grid architectures remain selectable. These are original encounter-scale deck sections inspired by the genre, not exact plans for named vessels or complete multi-deck starships.

**Windows in exterior cabins and lounges** adds glazing only to declared outer hull boundaries of quarters, lounges, offices, and briefing rooms. Inboard rooms, deck cut edges, and Borg compartments do not receive automatic windows. Structural piers separate panes. Foundry window walls block movement and sound while passing sight and light. Turning windows off restores solid hull walls without changing room placement. Ring and spine layouts also identify their exterior walls; painted layouts do not infer a hull from empty cells.

The new corridor carpet, sliding-door sill, and cabin window images are local assets selected automatically where applicable. Hull-section corridor trims follow the actual curved geometry rather than tiling a straight corridor picture around bends. Individual furniture is recommended for shaped rooms. Purpose-specific partitioned rooms and compact lifts use individual furnishings even when the pre-rendered kit is selected, so baked artwork cannot disagree with their walls. Other supported rooms can still use uniformly scaled kit insets. Sickbay furnishings leave a central approach aisle, and room labels stay upright on both sides of the passageway.

**Turbolifts** are compact rounded cabins attached directly to an existing corridor door, across all generated architectures. Hull sections place them on the inboard side of the passageway; ring decks place them inside the promenade, including with custom room selections. A lift-only hull section leaves its exterior room bays empty. The oversized lobby image is no longer used for these cabins.

**Crew quarters** reserve a bathroom against the cabin perimeter before placing furniture. Its floor follows the actual straight or curved bulkheads, with only the necessary internal partitions and a native sliding door. Corner arrangements leave an L-shaped living/sleeping area; shallow cabins can use an end bay. Bathrooms provide at least a 3 m × 3.6 m usable fixture/standing rectangle, plus any space following the hull. Standard bathrooms have a toilet, basin, and sonic shower. Larger suites can include a separate tub, with a larger reserved bathroom area. Beds and furnishings retain their physical size and stay outside the bathroom and door approaches. Quarters have one public corridor entrance; service hatches remain available where generated. Hull-section cabin bays and the ring's inner radius allow for the larger bathroom program. Pre-rendered kit mode uses individual furnishings for quarters so artwork matches the partitions. These apply to generated quarters; hand-painted rooms retain their painted architecture.

In hull sections, transporter rooms have a curved pad alcove and a separate operator station; cargo racks leave a central aisle. These arrangements follow the functional patterns in the user-provided room references, without copying their artwork or claiming to reproduce a named vessel exactly.

**Jefferies tubes and access hatches** adds a connected, one-square-wide maintenance route behind the hull section's inboard compartments, with branches into those rooms. Amber-edged hatches are native closed doors with narrower 0.7-square openings. Crawl with a smaller token. These are same-deck routes; vertical travel between scenes or decks is not automated. Lifts do not receive a rear service branch, and a lift-only section omits the crawlway. Other generated architectures retain their original circulation; automatic tube routing applies to hull sections. The brush offers a Jefferies tube paint type, but conversion to cells does not preserve the generator's fine cabin partitions or smooth walls.

**Rooms to include → Choose room types** lets you select the compartment types. Every selected type appears at least once, unchecked types are excluded, and larger sections repeat selected types. The generator grows small layouts when needed to fit the palette. Corridors are always supplied. With this control off, the department mix chooses the rooms. The **Room list** below the preview shows the resulting counts.

**Image assets** selects floor textures, consoles, beds, chairs, tables, cargo containers, couches, plants, office desks, coffee tables, diagnostic biobeds, and reactor cores. Fifteen original PNG assets are bundled in `assets/interiors`. Faction and era defaults choose carpet or metal plating and modern, retro, or imperial consoles. **Browse** accepts other image files from Foundry storage. Use transparent overhead PNG/WebP furniture and repeating floor textures; custom images must be at most 16 MB each. External URLs are not accepted. **Drawn detail** uses the procedural rendering for that slot.

**Curved radial walls** produces circular rooms and ring corridors with matching native wall segments. Short straight door landings keep sliding doors aligned with the artwork. Inner radial compartments span paired sectors to provide more usable space. Turn the option off for angular decks. Sickbays use diagnostic beds, engineering uses a reaction chamber and service trunks, and turbolifts show an open-front circular cabin with controls.

**Room artwork → Pre-rendered Starfleet TNG kit** uses nine additional finished room/corridor images: bridge, sickbay, engineering, crew quarters, turbolift lobby, lounge, office, straight corridor, and junction. Room images include their floor and furnishings and scale uniformly within the compartment, leaving an approach margin for native doors. The same kit works with generated and painted layouts. Operations uses the bridge kit and reactor control uses the engineering kit. Unsupported room types and tight spaces fall back to individual furniture. This kit is specifically Starfleet TNG artwork; other faction/era styles remain available through **Individual furniture assets**. Individual chair/bed/console selectors do not replace furniture already baked into a room-kit image. The room-kit choice is saved in the recipe as `roomKit: "starfleet"`; the default is `"assembled"`.

**Layout brush → Brush this layout** converts the generated floor plan to editable square cells. **Blank deck** starts an empty canvas at the selected deck size. Select a room type, passageway, or erase, then drag with a 1, 2, 4, or 8-square brush. Square and round brush footprints are available. A stroke starting inside a matching room extends that room; a stroke starting in empty space creates a separate room. Passageways join automatically. Leave at least two squares of shared wall between compartments for a sliding door. **Undo stroke** retains up to 40 edits, including blanking or returning to the generated layout. **Show artwork** previews the furnished result. Create Scene is disabled while rooms are disconnected or the map is empty. The brush supports concave rooms and empty shafts/cutouts; furniture stays inside an occupied rectangle. Painting uses grid edges, so converting a radial layout replaces its smooth curves with cell outlines. The generated radial mode remains available through **Return to generated layout**.

The preview uses the same geometry and selected assets as export. **Zoom 2×** enlarges the artwork for inspection; scroll inside the preview to inspect other areas. A seed and the same options reproduce a layout. **New random seed** produces a variant. Existing scene recipes can be loaded using **Use viewed scene’s recipe**. Room selection, asset paths, curved-wall selection, and all painted cells are included in the saved recipe. Painting takes precedence over the automatic department mix and room palette.

Version 4 replaces the small freestanding bathroom pods with perimeter-integrated wet areas and L-shaped living spaces, including tubs in larger suites. The inboard turbolift rule from version 3 remains. Reload Foundry after updating, then generate a new scene or load an older scene's recipe and create a variant. Existing scene backgrounds and walls are not automatically replaced. Hand-painted rooms retain their painted architecture.

**Create Scene** saves a WebP background under `worlds/<world-id>/sta2e-interiors/`, then creates a new scene with a 1.5-meter square grid, native walls, closed doors, and local lights. It opens for the GM without activating the scene for players. Place crew tokens in the circulation area at the initial view. Door thresholds are painted without a closed leaf so an opened Foundry door visibly remains passable. Props and labels are baked into the map artwork; walls, doors, and lights are editable Foundry documents. To change the furniture artwork or room palette, load the recipe and create a variant.

Foundry v14 uses a dedicated interior scene level; the v13 adapter uses a scene background. Background rendering is capped at 8192 pixels on its longest edge to bound memory. Scene coordinates and grid resolution remain unchanged. Furniture images are loaded once and embedded once in the SVG artwork, retaining their alpha. A missing or invalid image reports an error before scene creation. If storage upload succeeds but scene creation fails, its uniquely named image may remain in the world's interior folder for inspection or manual cleanup.

## Macros

```js
game.sta2eToolkit.openInteriorGenerator({
  faction: "federation", era: "tng", type: "starship", seed: "deck-47",
  roomTypes: ["bridge", "quarters", "medical", "transporter", "lounge"]
});

// Geometry only: no uploads or world document changes.
const layout = game.sta2eToolkit.generateInterior({ faction: "cardassian", type: "station", seed: "nor-12" });

// GM only: saves the artwork and creates a new scene.
await game.sta2eToolkit.createInteriorScene(layout.recipe, { name: "Cardassian Station", view: false });
```

Room keys: `bridge`, `ops`, `engineering`, `reactor`, `quarters`, `lab`, `medical`, `cargo`, `transporter`, `security`, `lounge`, `briefing`, `lift`, `airlock`, `regeneration`, `assimilation`, `workshop`, `office`. Set `roomTypes: null` to use the department mix. Architecture keys: `auto`, `section`, `spine`, `ring`, `lattice`. Set `hullWindows: false` for solid exterior walls and `jefferies: false` to omit the maintenance network. Tubes are infrastructure, not part of the selected room palette. Asset keys: `floor`, `corridor`, `threshold`, `window`, `console`, `bed`, `chair`, `table`, `crate`, `couch`, `plant`, `desk`, `coffee-table`, `biobed`, `core`; values are `auto`, `drawn`, or a relative Foundry image path.

For API use, `brush` is null for procedural geometry, or `{width, height, cells, kinds}`. Dimensions are integers from 8 to 128; `cells` contains `width * height` room IDs or null, and `kinds` maps IDs to room keys, `corridor`, or `jefferies`. Disconnected painted layouts are returned by the geometry API so an editor can display and repair them; `createInteriorScene` validates and rejects them before upload.

## Development verification

Run `node --experimental-vm-modules tests/interior-generation.mjs`. This covers 7,680 faction/era/type/size/department/architecture combinations, 500 room palettes, 60 varied brush maps plus targeted cutout and stroke fixtures, connectivity, wall and door geometry, curved arcs, reproducibility, saved brush round trips, real bundled image files and alpha, path validation, and v13/v14 scene data. Hull-section checks cover shaped-room furnishing bounds, private corridor access, hull-only glazing, window collision/sight settings, and disabling windows without changing the plan. Runtime checks also need the Foundry preview, file browser, image rendering, and scene creation workflow because document schemas and browser image decoding are not modeled by Node tests.

Version 3 regression checks additionally cover 200 compact-cabin and lift-palette combinations, bathrooms inside room boundaries, native bathroom doors, kit-mode bathroom visibility, and lift positions relative to the hull-section passageway and ring promenade. A standalone furnished rendering is available at [quarters-bathrooms-v3.webp](interior-previews/quarters-bathrooms-v3.webp). These checks passed; a new live Foundry check was unavailable because the local server refused connections during this correction.

Version 4 checks also verify perimeter contact, minimum bathroom space, fixture containment, native partition doors, full-size beds, and a complete division of the cabin into bathroom and living footprints without gaps. Targeted checks cover concave L-shaped living outlines and both standard and tub-equipped fixture sets. Furnished previews: [standard quarters](interior-previews/standard-quarters-v4.webp), [larger suite](interior-previews/suite-quarters-v4.webp), and [full hull section](interior-previews/quarters-v4-layout.webp). These previews are standalone renders of the generator, not replacements for existing Foundry scenes.

Artwork prompts and provenance are recorded beside the PNGs in `assets/interiors/README.md`, `console-variants.md`, `lounge-furniture.md`, `technical-furniture.md`, `room-kit.md`, `room-derived-assets.md`, and `hull-section-assets.md`. The 37 images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool and remain local assets during normal module use. The ten room-derived additions include helm and tactical consoles, crew and officer beds, command and visitor chairs, ready room and quarters desks, and two floors. Choose **Individual furniture assets** and the **TNG** choices in the asset dropdowns to use them. These variants preserve their image proportions within the generator's furnishing bounds; selecting one changes every use of that asset slot. They can also be placed manually as Foundry tiles. Open `assets/interiors/room-derived-catalog.html` locally for the catalog; Foundry serves HTML assets as plain text and blocks nested images in standalone SVG documents. The generator's embedded preview and exported WebP use the full artwork.

Runtime QA in Foundry v14 on 2026-09-13 verified room selection, the native image browser, furnished previews, brush strokes and undo, saving a painted scene, opening and closing its native sickbay door, and restoring its saved cells for further editing. The sample scene **Interior Example — Brushed Operations** contains a sickbay, office, lounge, engineering compartment, and turbolift. Automatic checks cover v13 scene data; a live v13 world was not available. The installed v14 renderer resizes level textures to the scene rectangle, preserving background/wall alignment when artwork is capped at 8192 pixels.

The v14 runtime also saved **Interior Example — Curved Starfleet Deck** and **Interior Example — Pre-rendered Starfleet Rooms**, and restored the latter's room-kit choice. Native door controls align with generated curved boundaries. Room-kit tests verify all seven room images are consumed, preserve their aspect ratio, and stay within furnishing frames; all nine kit files are checked as PNGs. The room-kit floor surrounds use measured corner colors and edge blending, while native walls are drawn in complete layers to keep curves continuous.

The furnished preview and saved WebP show a compact turbolift, divided exterior cabins, bathrooms in larger cabins, a transporter alcove, hull glazing, and four service hatches leading into a connected maintenance route.
