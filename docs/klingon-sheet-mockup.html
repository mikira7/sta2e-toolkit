Klingon actor sheets

The five 2e actor sheets — character, NPC, supporting, starship (Ship and Crew tabs) and small craft — in the Klingon Targeting Scope look the task card, chat cards and dice roller now wear under klingon: sections of dim red housing, every readout and list on a green-black phosphor scope with corner marks, the viewer's inverted triangle as each panel's lamp, tracks as rows of hexagonal cells, hexagonal selectors that light gold, and backlit plates cut wider at the top for keys and tabs. Hover a list row to bring up its edit and delete keys; the tracks and selectors are live.

    How this would be built

    Built: styles/klingon-sheet.css, tagged by tos-sheet-theme.js as .sta2e-kli-sheet and skipped by _applySheetTheme, as described below.

    The same route as the TOS, TMP and ENT overlays: a CSS overlay, never a template. tos-sheet-theme.js would stamp .sta2e-kli-sheet (one more entry in ERA_CLASSES, under isKlingonTheme) beside the data-sta2e-sheet="<kind>" stamp it already writes, _applySheetTheme would skip a tagged sheet as it skips the other three, and a styles/klingon-sheet.css would follow ent-sheet.css rule for rule. Nothing in the system's markup moves.

    What carries over from the chat card. The red housing, the green-black scope with scanlines, a faint reticle and two phosphor corner triangles, the head with its mono code and the inverted triangle lamp, the hexagonal cell the card's dice sit in, the triangle note lead, and the backlit plates. Chakra Petch for labels (already bundled in fonts/klingon/) and Share Tech Mono for every number (bundled in fonts/tmp/), so the overlay needs no new font.

    Housing or scope. The panels are the template's own columns and become housing. Everything that reads out — the attribute and discipline blocks, the systems and departments, every item list, the portrait and the four ship stats — becomes a scope. Typed fields (the name, the character tab, the service record) are thin scope strips with a phosphor underline, so typed text is always phosphor on black and labels are always salmon on red. The head's ticks and tab are pseudo-elements in a build, as the ENT overlay's are.

    Hexagons are fills, not borders. A clip-path cuts off a border, so an unlit track cell is a dark fill and a selector is a rim fill under an inset second hexagon — the same construction as the roller's indicators. Track cells stay numbered, which is why they are hexagons rather than the card's triangle meter: a triangle has no room for a two-digit number.

    Colour by meaning. Reputation and Shields are phosphor green, Determination gold, Stress red, Crew Support amber, and the NPC's Personal Threat amber through the data-sta2e-sheet stamp (it reuses box stress). Selected attributes, disciplines, systems and departments turn gold; non-zero breaches turn red through .breaches:not([value="0"]), as on the other overlays.

    The traps the other overlays carry apply here too. sta.css gives several blocks width: 100% as a content box (list headers, .buttons, .text-entry, the supporting improvement rows), so any padding added must come with box-sizing: border-box; and sta.css forces some border colours with !important, so the underlines would be box-shadows in the build.
